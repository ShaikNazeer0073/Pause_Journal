# Pause_Journal

> **Give your day a place to land.**

Pause is a simple daily journaling experience built around one small habit:

**Rate your day → Write it down → Save it → Come back tomorrow.**

The goal is to make journaling feel calm, personal, and effortless without
adding unnecessary complexity.

## Live Demo

**[pausejournal.vercel.app](https://pausejournal.vercel.app/)**

## What is Pause?

Pause gives you a simple space to record how your day felt and what happened.

Each daily entry lets you:

- Rate your day from 1–5 stars
- Write a personal journal entry
- Choose a mood
- Save your entry
- Browse previous entries
- Edit entries later

The interface is intentionally focused on the writing experience rather
than turning journaling into a complicated dashboard.
