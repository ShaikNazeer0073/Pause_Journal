# Decisions

## Product
Pause is a simple and easy-to-use daily journaling product that allows
users to rate their day on a scale from 1 to 5 stars and write down a brief
diary entry.

## Reasons for this design
Since journaling is a highly personal activity, I went for a simple and calming
visual design. The interface is focused on the user's writing rather than adding extra features.

## Interaction
The key interactions in the product are rating users' days from 1 to 5 stars and saving
a diary entry. Previously saved entries can also be edited.

## Responsive Design
This product's layout was made with mobile and desktop use in mind. On small screens,
journal editor and history become vertical and controls are touch-friendly.

## Performance
There are no heavy libraries or other assets required in the project - just plain
HTML, CSS and JavaScript code.

## Accessibility
Semantically correct HTML code, good contrast ratios, focus states and keyboard-accessible controls are implemented.

## Tradeoffs
I purposely left the frontend of the prototype without any additional authentication or backend for the sake of simplicity.
